# ui-workshop scope

The same stages as `ui-first` (the 26 core `workshop` stages, the five Ideation
stages intent-capture, feasibility, scope-definition, rough-mockups and
approval-handoff, and the `ui-freeze` gate), tuned for one workshop table that has
about an hour of agent time and a small budget.

What differs from `ui-first`:

- depth `Minimal`: the person's intake card answers most questions, so stages
  skip what it already says;
- `summary_confirmation: off`: no separate "Looks correct" read-back turn before
  each stage writes its artifacts;
- `learnings: off`, `collaborators: off`, `review_cap: none`: no stage diary,
  no support agents, no stage reviewer.

What does not change: every approval gate still asks the person, human presence
stays on (`guard_policy: off` lowers plan-approval and freeze fences for
undirected work but keeps human presence up), `plan_approval: on`, `sensors: on`,
and `ui-freeze` still sits before every Construction stage.

## Membership

Keyword triggers: `ui-workshop`, `ui workshop`, `workshop prototype`. Pick it with
`/aidlc ui-workshop "<intake card>"` or `/aidlc --scope ui-workshop "<intake card>"`.
Membership of core stages is declared by this plugin's contributions
(`adds.scopes: [ui-first, ui-workshop]`, generated by `addon/tools/gen_contributions.py`);
`ui-freeze` declares both scopes in its own `scopes:`.
Market research and team formation stay skipped, as in `workshop`.
