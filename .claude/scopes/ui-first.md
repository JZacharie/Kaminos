# ui-first scope

Runs everything the core `workshop` scope runs (initialization, the full
inception design pass, construction, operation) plus five Ideation stages:
intent-capture, feasibility, scope-definition, rough-mockups and
approval-handoff. The `rough-mockups` stage additionally writes a clickable
`prototype.html` and a `ui-map.json`. The `ui-freeze` gate stage sits after the
last Inception stage and before every Construction stage, so the build only
starts once the person has confirmed the prototype.

## Membership

Keyword triggers: `ui-first`, `ui first`, `prototype first`, `clickable prototype`.
Membership of core stages is declared by this plugin's contributions
(`adds.scopes: [ui-first]`, generated by `addon/tools/gen_contributions.py`);
`ui-freeze` declares the scope in its own `scopes:`.
Market research and team formation stay skipped, as in `workshop`.
