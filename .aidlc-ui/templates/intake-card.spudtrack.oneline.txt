SpudTrack: a web app for Patatas Doradas, a potato-chip maker, to follow shipments to retailers. Users: the logistics coordinator (daily work) and the operations manager (KPIs). It prevents late deliveries and spoiled product going unnoticed. First version, 5 screens: sign-in (email and password, one demo user); shipments list (table with filters by status on time/delayed/delivered, retailer and date); shipment detail (route stops, expected vs. actual arrival, temperature chart); alerts (delayed and temperature alerts, newest first, acknowledge button); KPI dashboard (on-time rate, average delay, open alerts, shipments per day). Data: mock JSON only (about 40 shipments, 6 retailers, hourly temperature readings), no database, no carrier integration. Must-haves: filter shipments; show delay in hours once the expected arrival has passed; temperature alert when a reading stays above 8 C for more than 30 minutes; KPIs computed from the same mock data. Out of scope: real sign-up and roles, GPS or carrier integration, e-mail or SMS, invoices, mobile app. Constraints: English labels, browser only (no server code), clickable demo in 45 minutes, one primary colour (potato gold).
